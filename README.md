## 🐺 What is UNCAGED?

UNCAGED is a premium club night founded by **Aphex** — a DJ and performer with roots in pup and furry events across Europe including PAWSOME, Doggy Weekend, and ANIMALZ BERLIN.

We brought that energy home to the South West. Whether you're a seasoned pup, a curious furry, or just someone who loves heavy electronic music and wants a night where you can truly be yourself — **you belong here.**

> You do not need to wear a pup hood or fursuit to attend. Come as you are.

## 🏳️‍🌈 Our Values

- **Safe space** — dedicated changing area, staffed cloakroom, private outdoor courtyard
